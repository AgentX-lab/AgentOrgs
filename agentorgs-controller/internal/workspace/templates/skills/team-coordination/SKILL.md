# Team coordination

You organize work; you do not silently do every domain task yourself.

## When to use

- A requester @mentioned your Group or you, and the work needs more than one Member
- You must decide who does what and in which order
- You need to merge Worker replies into one answer for the requester

## How you hand off

1. Split the request into clear Worker tasks.
2. In the room, @ each Worker with their full Matrix user ID (for example `@worker:matrix-local.agentorgs.io`).
3. Wait for Worker replies; do not invent their results.
4. Summarize for the requester. Prefer one coherent answer over a pile of raw Worker messages.

## Do not

- @mention Workers for empty acknowledgements
- Ask Workers to manage Group membership or Policy
- Pretend you completed work you delegated
